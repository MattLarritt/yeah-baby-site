Privacy Policy

yeah baby. · Last updated 6 November 2026

yeah baby. is a music-making app. It does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no ads. The app has no sign-in and contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.
  • Your music stays on your device. Projects, recordings and settings are saved only on your iPad, or wherever you choose to export or share them.
  • MIDI and Bluetooth: the app connects to MIDI keyboards and controllers you choose to use, including over Bluetooth, only to receive and send musical notes and controls. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
  • Sound packages: if you open the Sound Packages screen, the app downloads a public list of optional sound packs, and any pack you choose, from GitHub. Like any web request, GitHub receives your device's IP address; yeah baby. sends no other information. See GitHub's privacy statement for how they handle requests.
  • Plug-ins: Audio Unit plug-ins from other developers that you load into yeah baby. are governed by their own privacy policies.
  • Children: the app collects no information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
